What it tracks

Every animal you raise,
every dollar it costs.

Haymow is built around your operation. Start from a common preset or configure your own — dairy, poultry, meat birds, or anything else you raise.

Common presets included:

🐄
Dairy

AM and PM milking sessions, yield tracking against lactation curve, feed-to-output correlation for cows, goats, sheep, or any milking animal.

🥚
Layer Flocks

Daily egg counts, lay rate %, soft-shell and broken tracking. Cost per dozen calculated automatically from your feed costs.

🐔
Meat Birds

Chick-to-freezer lifecycle. Weekly weight samples, mortality log, and cost per lb at harvest.

🌾
Feed Inventory

Track stock on hand, cost per unit, and low-stock alerts. Each session log automatically deducts from inventory.

⚙️
Fully Configurable

Not just the presets. Define your own animal types, production metrics, and feed categories to match exactly how you run your operation.

📈
Feed-to-Yield Correlation

Automatically correlates what an animal ate with what it produced. Spot patterns across 7, 30, or 90 days for any species you track.

💰
Real Economics

Cost per gallon of milk, cost per dozen eggs, cost per lb of meat — calculated automatically from your actual feed costs and yields.

How it works

Log from anywhere.
Understand everything.

Designed for the person with muddy boots and a phone in their pocket — not for someone sitting at a desk.

1
Set up your animals once

Add your cow, your layer flock, or your meat bird batch. One guided setup — takes about two minutes.

2
Tap to log any session

Big buttons, three taps maximum. Enter yield, feed, and any health notes — then you're back to work.

3
Watch the trends build

Every session feeds your lactation curve, lay rate chart, and cost calculations automatically. The data works for you.

4
Text from the barn (coming soon)

Text a number from your barn or coop — "3.2 gal AM, 3lb grain" — and Haymow logs it and texts a confirmation back. No app required.
